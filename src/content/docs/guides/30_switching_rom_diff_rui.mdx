## Slight explanation

realme UI 2.0 and realme UI 3.0 based ROMs both include
firmware in their flashable package. Therefore, you can
flash the ROM and you'll automatically be on that realme
UI firmware. The only extra step is you have to flash
custom recovery made for that realme UI version.

## Steps

**Suppose you're in RUI 2 ROM, and you want to switch to
RUI 3 ROM.**

<Steps>

1. Make sure you have downloaded the RUI 3 ROM **and**
   RUI 3 custom recovery.

1. Boot into your existing custom recovery. Flash the
   RUI 3 ROM that you desire.

1. Flash custom recovery for RUI 3.

1. If you're clean flashing, format your data.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
Do not reboot in between these steps. Only after you
have done all the steps, you may proceed to reboot your
device.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip">
If you instead want to switch from RUI 3 ROM to RUI 2
ROM, the step is the same. Just replace RUI 3 ROM and
RUI 3 recovery in the steps above with RUI 2.
</Aside>
