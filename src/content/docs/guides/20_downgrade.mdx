## Downgrade Guide

<Steps>

1. Download the right rollback package for your device.
   - [realme 7](https://t.me/rm6785dumppAss/1104)
   - [realme Narzo 20 Pro](https://t.me/rm6785dumppAss/1105)
   - [realme Narzo 30 4G](https://t.me/rm6785dumppAss/1106)

1. Go to about phone, tap build number 7 times to
   enable Developer Options.

1. Now go to about phone and tap the realme UI 3 banner.

1. Click the 3 dots at the top right corner and choose
   local install; find the rollback package that you've
   just downloaded.

</Steps>
