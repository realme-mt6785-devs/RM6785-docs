<Aside type="caution">
Please read every section thoroughly before doing anything to
your device. Make sure you understand how to do each of the steps!
</Aside>

<Aside type="note">
Before proceeding with bootloader unlock, you must be in
realme UI 2.0. If you are on realme UI 3.0, follow this
[downgrade](../20_downgrade) guide to go back to realme
UI 2.0. Similarly, if you're on realme UI 1.0, please update
to realme UI 2.0 via settings. If the update does not show
up, follow [this troubleshooting steps](../../troubleshooting/rui2).
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. Turn on developer options and enable OEM unlock.

1. Download [mtkclient](https://t.me/rm6785dumppAss/1079). You
   might need to disable Windows' real time protection. Extract
   the zip and install usbdk inside the extracted folder.

1. Open mtk gui 2.0 and do not close it.

1. Power off your phone. Press vol up and vol down simultaneously
   and connect it to the PC. DO NOT release the volume buttons
   until connection succeeds.

   <Aside type="tip">
   The volume key must be pressed BEFORE connecting it to the PC,
   and keep the key pressed while connecting it. Once it succeeds,
   the key may be released.
   </Aside>

1. In mtkclient, go to 'Flash Tools' section and click
   'Unlock Bootloader' button.

1. Go to 'Erase Partition' section and erase the following
   partitions:

   ```
   md_udc
   metadata
   userdata
   ```

</Steps>

<Aside type="danger">
Step 5 & 6 will ERASE YOUR DATA. Make a backup before proceeding.
</Aside>

## Enabling fastboot

Unlike official bootloader unlock method (which is dead), unlocking
with mtkclient doesn't make fastboot work out of the box. Patched lk
is needed to make fastboot work. `lk` stands for 'little kernel',
which is what MTK uses as the bootloader.

<sub>
_Technically, you can achieve similar result of unlocking by official
method. Just ask for `seccfg` dump from someone whose bootloader was
unlocked via official method, and flash it to your device with
mtkclient. You don't even need to unlock first via mtkclient. The
benefit of this (and thus the prior official method) is that you will
not suffer from dm-verity corruption and therefore don't need patched
LKs. However this hasn't been tested and you should not do this if
you're a beginner._
</sub>

### Download the appropriate lk for your device

[click this for list of patched lks](../../appendix/lk)

### Steps for flashing lk

<Steps>

1. Open mtk gui 2.0 and do not close it.

1. Power off your phone. Press vol up and vol down simultaneously
   and connect it to the PC. DO NOT release the volume buttons
   until connection succeeds.

   <Aside type="tip">
   The volume key must be pressed BEFORE connecting it to the PC,
   and keep the key pressed while connecting it. Once it succeeds,
   the key may be released.
   </Aside>

1. Go to 'Write Partition' section. Set the downloaded lk file
   for lk partition and flash. Do not touch lk2.

1. Disconnect cable and hold all buttons until fastboot appears.
   You can now flash recovery you wish to use.

</Steps>

## Flashing Recovery

For a list of recoveries and their link, check out
[recoveries](../../appendix/recoveries) section.

### Recovery flashing guide

<Steps>

1. Boot to fastboot. Power off phone and hold all
   three buttons until fastboot appears.

1. Connect your device and verify it via `fastboot devices`.

1. Now the flashing part:

   ```
   fastboot flash recovery <drag and drop recovery file>
   ```

1. Reboot into recovery:

   ```
   fastboot reboot recovery
   ```

1. Now you can flash whatever you want to.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
If you do nothing in recovery and reboot back into
realme UI, your custom recovery will be overwritten
and it will become realme UI recovery again. You will
need to repeat the process all over again.
</Aside>
